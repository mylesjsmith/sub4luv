  1. Open Prime Gaming on Twitch

    Tap Connect Prime Account.

  2. Log in to Twitch

    Log in, or tap Sign up to make a free Twitch account if you don't have one.

  3. Link your Amazon account

    Sign in with the Amazon account that has Prime, then tap Confirm.

  4. Search “Sub4luvv”

    Search for Sub4luvv (two v's) and open the channel.

  5. Subscribe with Prime 💜

    Tap Subscribe, make sure Use Prime Sub is checked, then tap Subscribe with Prime. It's free, and you just helped someone in need.

⏰ Set a monthly reminder

Prime subs don't renew on their own. Your free sub refreshes every 30 days, so come back and re-sub to Sub4luvv each month to keep helping.
