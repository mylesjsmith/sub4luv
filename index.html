Our mission

To educate people about their free benefits that can fund projects that help people who really need it. We strive to build a community that takes advantage of online media and turn it into real world impact.

If you've never used Twitch: Twitch is a live streaming site. If you pay for Amazon Prime, part of what you're already paying for is one free subscription to a Twitch channel every month. Most people never use it, so it just sits there unused. Sub4luv is a channel. Give it your free sub instead and $2.25 goes toward real world support for people who need it, and it costs you nothing.

How to give your free sub

No credit card involved, ever. You're just pointing a benefit you already have somewhere it can help.

1. Visit our Twitch page

Head to twitch.tv/subs/sub4luvv, that's our channel's subscribe page.

Go to twitch.tv/subs/sub4luvv

2. Connect your Amazon Prime

If Twitch and Amazon aren't linked yet, it'll ask you to log in and connect them. That's what unlocks your free sub.

3. Use your free sub

Choose "Subscribe with Prime." Confirm it. No card is involved at any point, you're just using the free sub Prime already gives you.

Doesn't auto renew monthly. Next time it's just one click, no need to redo the other steps. Follow us on Instagram for reminders and updates.
